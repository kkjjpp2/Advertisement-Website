My Story

I've loved creating for a long time, and I've put in multiple years of hands-on work. That experience shapes the way I think about advertising for businesses.

Today, Jae's Auto Media runs Facebook, Instagram and targeted Google advertising for businesses of all kinds. Smaller businesses compete against bigger names, and targeted ads give any business a direct way to put what it offers in front of the right people, whether they are scrolling social media or searching on Google.

I handle the campaigns so you can focus on what you do best, which is running your business. When you work with me you deal with Jae directly, so you get straight answers, quick replies and advertising shaped around your business instead of a one-size-fits-all package.
